How do we measure results?

Before we change anything, we take a Week-1 baseline: the same set of buyer questions run on all 8 engines, with every answer, every brand named and every source cited stored with its date. That baseline is the line every later number is measured against.

Then every fix we ship is logged with its date and re-measured. We report weekly against the baseline, so you can see what moved and what did not.

What are the two scoreboards?

We report on both, because AI visibility only matters if it reaches the business.

  • Scoreboard 1: the AI answers

    Visibility score, citation rate, share of voice and brand sentiment, measured on every stored answer across the 8 engines.

  • Scoreboard 2: SEO and owned enquiries

    Search performance and the enquiries that reach your own channels, traced back to where demand began: an AI answer, a review, a thread or a video.

Two programmes in detail

Real estate: 32% to 58% in under 2.5 months

A residential developer in India, measured across 8 AI engines. The visibility score rose from 32% to 58% from the Week-1 baseline. Citation rate, share of voice and brand sentiment went up, and the developer now shows up for 80–90 tracked buyer questions.

Investor relations: from old news to the latest filing

A listed company in India. The AI answer to investor questions moved from old figures and a resolved controversy to the latest filing. Answer accuracy and current sources cited went up; the old controversy as headline went down.

How does a result get made?

  1. 01

    Scan

    Run the buyer questions on 8 engines and record who is named, in what order, quoting which source.

  2. 02

    Diagnose

    Find the gaps: wrong facts, missing pages, the sources engines trust and the rivals named instead.

  3. 03

    Fix

    Ship AI-citable pages, FAQs, schema, review replies and PR. Every fix is logged with its date.

  4. 04

    Prove

    Once published and crawled, re-measure and report weekly against the Week-1 baseline.

Why we never name clients: the work we do touches how a brand is described, and that belongs to the brand. So we do not name, describe identifiably, count or quote our clients, and we show no logos or testimonials. Our case studies describe the sector and the market, never the company.

What we won't promise: guaranteed rankings. The engines decide what they say. We do the work rigorously and show every week what moved.
