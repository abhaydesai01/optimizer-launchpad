Results · Real estate

32% to 58% in AI answers.

A residential developer in India, measured across 8 AI engines from a Week-1 baseline. Under 2.5 months later, the brand appeared in 58% of tracked answers.

10 Nov 20266 min readBy the Optimizer360 team

The short answer

In this programme, a residential developer in India raised its visibility score from 32% to 58% across 8 AI engines in under 2.5 months, measured from the Week-1 baseline. Citation rate, share of voice and brand sentiment all went up, and the developer now shows up for 80–90 tracked buyer questions. The work combined site pages, communities, reference sources, press and video.

We publish two figures from this programme: the visibility score and the number of prompts ranked. The other measures are shown as direction only. Prompts ranked is one parameter, not the headline: the visibility score is the measure that counts across all 8 engines.

What did we do on the site?

  • Answer-first blogs and buyer guides.
  • Project fact pages with RERA numbers, possession dates, sizes and amenities.
  • FAQ and comparison pages for the questions buyers ask.
  • Schema for the company, every project and every FAQ.
  • Real numbers engines can quote, such as the delivery record and homes handed over.

What did we do in communities?

  • Reddit: real answers in city and home-buying threads, posted openly as the brand.
  • Quora answers to the same buyer questions.
  • Replies to reviews on Google and on property portals.

What did we do on reference sources?

  • Wikipedia: facts corrected through the talk page, with sources. Never promotional edits.
  • Wikidata: one clean entity for the company and its projects.
  • Google Business Profiles fixed for every project.
  • Property portal listings made consistent.

What did we do in press and video?

  • Digital PR in property and business media.
  • "Best of" lists and local area guides.
  • YouTube site walkthroughs with full transcripts.

How are the numbers defined?

  • Visibility score: the share of tracked questions, across all 8 engines, where the brand appears in the answer.
  • Citation rate: the share of answers that cite the brand's own pages as a source.
  • Share of voice: the brand's share of all brand mentions in category answers, against the rivals named.
  • Brand sentiment: the tone of how engines describe the brand, scored on every stored answer.
  • Prompts ranked: the tracked buyer questions where the brand now shows up in the answer.

How did the programme run?

  1. 01

    Scan

    Took the Week-1 baseline: the buyer question universe run on all 8 engines, every answer and source stored with its date.

  2. 02

    Diagnose

    Found which rivals were named instead, which portals, forums and media the engines trusted, and where facts were missing or wrong.

  3. 03

    Fix

    Shipped the site pages, community answers, reference corrections, PR and video, each change logged with its date.

  4. 04

    Prove

    Re-measured every week against the baseline, on the AI answers and on SEO and owned enquiries.

What this is not: a promise. The engines decide what they say. This is one programme, measured the same way every week. We do not name the developer.

Questions

How long did it take to go from 32% to 58% visibility?

Under 2.5 months from the Week-1 baseline, measured across 8 AI engines. This is one programme; timelines depend on the category, the starting point and the sources engines already trust.

What is a visibility score?

It is the share of tracked questions, across all 8 engines, where the brand appears in the answer. It is the headline measure because it covers every engine and every question we track.

Did you edit Wikipedia for the developer?

No promotional edits. We proposed factual corrections on the Wikipedia talk page, with sources, for editors to review. We also kept one clean Wikidata entity for the company and its projects.

Who is the developer?

We never name clients. It is a residential developer in India, and that is all we share publicly.

Why are only two figures published?

We publish the visibility score and prompts ranked as figures. Citation rate, share of voice and brand sentiment are shown as direction only, so the page stays accurate without exposing client data.
