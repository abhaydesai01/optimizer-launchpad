# "Own it" pillars: proof lines used on the page (verified Nov 2026)

Why now
- Pew Research (Jul 2025): with a Google AI summary, users clicked a result on 8% of visits vs 15% without; 1% clicked a link inside the summary.
- SparkToro/Datos (2024): 58.5% of US Google searches end without a click.
- G2 (May 2025, 1,169 B2B buyers): 29% start research on AI chat tools more often than Google.
- Gartner (Feb 2024) PREDICTS 25% drop in traditional search volume by 2026 (forecast).
- ChatGPT: 800M weekly users (Oct 2025); India 100M weekly users (Feb 2026).

1 Brand story: Profound (2025, 680M citations): Wikipedia = 47.9% of ChatGPT's top-10 source share. Semrush (2025): Wikipedia and Reddit are ChatGPT's two most-cited domains.
2 Reputation: BrightLocal (2025): 74% of consumers use 2+ review sites; only 4% never read reviews. Justdial Q4 FY26: 157.1M ratings & reviews.
3 Community: Peec AI (Mar 2026, 30M sources): Reddit is the #1 directly cited source across ChatGPT, AI Mode, Gemini, Perplexity, AI Overviews. Reddit/SurveyMonkey 2026 (Reddit-sponsored): 73% of B2B decision-makers trust peer recommendations vs 39% AI chatbots.
4 Feeder network: Ahrefs (2025, 75K brands): brand mentions correlate 0.664 with AI Overview visibility vs 0.218 for backlinks (correlation). Google-Kantar India car buyers (2026): 93% used AI in research; ~6 in 10 said YouTube shaped the shortlist.
5 Conversations: HBR (2011): replying within an hour = ~7x more likely to qualify the lead. Workato (Mar 2026, 114 B2B firms): 99%+ didn't respond within 5 minutes; avg email reply 11h 54m.
6 Pipeline: 6sense (Oct 2024): buyers ~70% through the process before talking to sellers. Responsive (Oct 2025): ~2/3 of B2B buyers use GenAI as much as or more than search for vendor research.

Caveats: citation shares move fast, date-stamp them; Gartner is a forecast; HBR is 2011; vendor studies = "studies show".
