FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

What we do, how we work, what we will not do, and how to start.

The short answer

Optimizer360 is an AI-native organic marketing company that helps brands get named, cited and described accurately in AI answers, search, media and communities. We track 8 AI engines, work in 10 industries, start every programme with a Week-1 baseline and report weekly. We never promise rankings, never name clients and never post fake reviews. The questions below cover the rest.

Short answers to the questions buyers ask us most. For definitions of the terms used here, see the GEO glossary. For anything not covered, email abhay@optimizer360.ai.

Questions

What does Optimizer360 do?

We make brands found, trusted, followed, talked about and chosen in AI answers, search, media and communities. AI agents track how engines describe you, and our expert teams fix what is missing or wrong, from pages and schema to reviews, PR and community answers.

What is generative engine optimisation (GEO)?

GEO is the practice of making a brand easier for AI engines to find, trust, name and cite in their answers. The US spelling is generative engine optimization. It covers what you publish, the sources engines rely on and how accurately you are described.

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO aims to rank pages in a list of search results. GEO aims to get your brand named, cited and described accurately inside the answer itself. The two share foundations, which is why we report on both scoreboards: the AI answers, and SEO plus owned enquiries.

Which AI engines do you track?

Eight: ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Grok, Perplexity, Kimi, Microsoft Copilot and Google AI Overviews. We track up to 1,000 prompts per brand a day and store every answer with its date.

How long does GEO take to show results?

It depends on your category, starting point and the sources engines already trust. In one real-estate programme, a residential developer in India moved from a 32% to a 58% visibility score across 8 engines in under 2.5 months. That is one programme, not a promise.

How much does GEO cost?

It depends on scope: the brands, markets and questions involved. We do not publish prices. Most clients start with a Week-1 baseline, which gives a scorecard, a ranked question universe and a costed plan.

Do we have to sign a long contract?

No. There are three ways to start: a baseline and universe map; owning your strongest questions first; or a one-quarter pilot with both scoreboards reported monthly and a clear go or stop at month 3.

Do you guarantee rankings in ChatGPT or Google?

No. The engines decide what they say, and anyone who guarantees rankings is guessing. We do the work rigorously, measure from a Week-1 baseline and show every week what moved.

How do you measure and report results?

We take a Week-1 baseline, log every fix with its date and re-measure after it is published and crawled. Weekly reports compare against the baseline on two scoreboards: the AI answers, and SEO plus owned enquiries.

Do you name your clients or show testimonials?

No. We never name, describe identifiably, count or quote clients, and we show no logos or testimonials. Our case studies describe the sector and the market only.

Do you post on Reddit or edit Wikipedia for clients?

On Reddit and Quora we post openly as the brand, never through fake accounts. On Wikipedia we only propose factual corrections on talk pages, with sources, and never make promotional edits.

Do you write fake reviews?

Never. We help brands earn a steady stream of real reviews and reply to every one in public. No fabricated reviews, figures or astroturfing.

How do you handle compliance in India?

Brand, legal and regulatory sign-off comes before anything goes live. We check against RERA, ASCI and SEBI rules where they apply, and our work is aligned with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP).

Can you help a listed company with what AI tells investors?

Yes, through our Investor Relations package. We keep AI answers accurate and current using only public disclosures, follow SEBI rules and get company sign-off before anything is published. Accuracy, not spin.

Are you an agency or a software tool?

We are an agency that runs on our own platform, Optimizer GEO. The platform tracks the engines, answers and sources; our six specialist teams ship the fixes and own the KPIs.

Which industries do you work in?

Nine: real estate and hospitality; financial services and investor relations; retail and consumer brands; food and FMCG; B2B and professional services; technology and SaaS; pharma and healthcare; manufacturing; industrial and engineering; cinema and entertainment.

Which countries do you work in?

Our home market is India. We also work in the United States and Mauritius.

What do we get in the first week?

A Week-1 baseline: a readiness scorecard across 8 factors, a ranked universe of the questions your buyers ask, and a costed plan. It is the line every later result is measured against.

How do we get started?

Email abhay@optimizer360.ai or request an AI audit on our contact page. It shows which engines name you, who they name instead, the sources they cite and the first fixes we would make.
