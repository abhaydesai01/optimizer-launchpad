Mentioned vs Cited in AI Answers: The 4 States Your Brand Can Be In

AI engines often name your brand without linking it, or link it without naming it. See the 4 states and how to fix each.

23 Nov 202614 min readBy the Optimizer360 team

The short answer

A mention is when an AI engine names your brand in its answer. A citation is when it links your page as a source. They rarely happen together: in a June 2026 Semrush study of 3,981 domain appearances, only 13.2% had both. Track each one separately, by engine, or your AI visibility numbers will mislead you.

What is the difference between a mention and a citation in AI answers?

A mention is an AI engine naming your brand in the text of its answer. A citation is a source the engine links to or names as the basis for its answer. A mention gives you credit with the reader. A citation gives the reader a page to check. Either can happen without the other.

Why does an engine mention a brand without citing it, or the reverse?

Because the two come from different steps. A mention comes from the model linking your brand to a category, either from its training data or from text it read while answering. A citation needs two retrieval steps: the engine fetches a page, then credits it as a source. A brand can clear the first and fail the second.

Adobe's brand visibility documentation draws the line this way: “A mention names your brand in the text; a citation links your page as a source.” It adds that a mention can come from training data with no retrieval step, while a citation comes from retrieval.

The engines' own documentation shows the second step is not automatic.

  • Perplexity says each answer includes “numbered citations linking to the original sources”.
  • OpenAI's web search tool returns inline citations for URLs found in search results. Its documentation separates the two ideas: the sources field lists the URLs the model consulted, and “the number of sources is often greater than the number of citations.”
  • Google describes AI Mode as giving a response “with links to supporting websites”, and says pages must be eligible to be shown as a supporting link.

Ahrefs measured the gap on the page side. Across 1.4 million ChatGPT prompts, only about half (49.98%) of the URLs ChatGPT retrieved were cited in the answer (Ahrefs, April 2026). So a page can be consulted and never cited, and a brand can be mentioned with no page behind it. In a University of Toronto preprint, Chen and Koudas found that 46% of ChatGPT's mentions of a brand the question did not name had no cited page that named it.

What are the four states a brand can be in?

For any buyer question, your brand is in one of four states, depending on whether it is mentioned and whether one of your pages is cited. This is our own framing, not an industry standard. Check each question, in each engine, on its own.

StateWhat you seeWhat it may meanFirst thing to check
1. Mentioned and citedYour name is in the answer and a page on your site is cited.The engine both trusts the claim and found a source for it on your pages.Is what it says accurate and current? Keep it that way.
2. Mentioned, not citedYour name is in the answer. The citations go to other sites, or there are none.The engine knows of you, but the evidence came from another source, or from its training.Which pages did it cite instead? Often a directory, review site or news piece.
3. Cited, not mentionedYour page is cited, but the text of the answer does not mention your name. This is a ghost citation.Your page supplied a fact. Another brand or a generic answer got the credit.Does your page say plainly who you are, next to the fact?
4. AbsentYour name is not mentioned and your page is not cited.The engine did not find a reason to include you for this question.Who is mentioned instead, and what do their pages say that yours do not?

Each state calls for a different fix. A brand in state 2 does not need more pages of its own. It needs to know where the engine finds its evidence, and whether that evidence is correct. A brand in state 3 may already have good content that fails to say whose it is. A brand in state 4 has a larger problem.

Which happens more often, a mention without a citation or the reverse?

Across all engines, a citation without a mention is more common, but it depends on the engine. In a Semrush study with Kevin Indig's Growth Memo (June 2026) of 3,981 domain appearances, 61.7% were ghost citations, 25.1% were mentions with no citation, and 13.2% were both.

The engines split in opposite directions. ChatGPT cited sources in 87% of appearances and mentioned the brand in 20.7%. Gemini mentioned the brand in 83.7% and cited sources in 21.4%. An average across engines hides which state you are in where.

Which AI engines mention brands most, and which cite most?

Gemini and Google AI Mode lean toward mentions. ChatGPT leans toward citations. Google AI Overviews sits between them. For Claude and Copilot we found no published split, so test them yourself. Check every engine your buyers use, separately.

Does the type of question change whether a brand is mentioned or cited?

Yes. Comparison questions draw the most brand mentions, and informational questions draw the most citations. In the Semrush study, comparison queries had a 43.3% mention rate, about 2.4 times the informational rate of 18%, while informational queries had an 89.3% citation rate.

How-to queries had a 42.8% mention rate. Commercial queries had a 35.6% mention rate and an 84.4% citation rate. For a buyer choosing between vendors, that matters: the comparison question is where being mentioned counts most, and it is also where a brand is easiest to leave out.

How do I get my brand mentioned in AI answers?

Get your brand named consistently by sources other than your own site. Engines connect a brand to a category from the wider web, so the evidence has to exist outside your pages. This is our working method, not a measured recipe.

  • Use one name. Write your brand, products and category the same way on your site, profiles and press mentions.
  • Earn independent coverage. News, industry publications and analyst pieces that describe what you do in a sentence an engine can lift.
  • Be present where buyers compare. Review sites, directories and community threads such as Reddit, where real customers describe you in their own words.
  • Get into “best X” lists honestly. Comparison questions draw the most mentions, so list pages that include you matter. Earn the place with accurate facts. Do not pay for placement and call it coverage.
  • Fix wrong facts first. A mention with an error does harm. See when AI gets your brand wrong.

How do I get my page cited in AI answers?

Make each section of the page answer one question on its own, so the engine can lift it and credit it. The KDD 2024 generative engine optimization paper found that adding quotations, statistics and citations to content raised visibility in generative answers by up to 40%, while keyword stuffing fell below baseline.

  • Answer first. Open every section with a 30 to 60 word answer that makes sense when quoted alone.
  • Use statistics with full sources. Name the study, the date and the sample.
  • Put the main content in the HTML. If the answer loads only through a script, a crawler may not see it.
  • Let AI crawlers in. Check robots rules and firewalls for the crawlers of the engines you care about.
  • Add structured data. Organization, Article and FAQ markup state plainly who you are and what the page says.
  • Say who you are next to the fact. This is the fix for state 3, shown next.

Do AI citations drive traffic?

Some, but AI referrals are still a small share of site visits. SE Ranking found AI platforms sent 0.32% of website traffic from January to April 2026, across 101,574 sites (SE Ranking). A US study by Conductor, reported by Search Engine Land, found 1.08% of visits in May to September 2025 (Conductor). Both are vendor studies and they used different samples.

So do not judge mentions and citations by clicks alone. A mention puts your name in front of a buyer who may search for you later, and that never shows up as an AI referral. Track both measures as visibility, and track visits as a separate result.

Does Google Search Console show AI citations?

In part. Google's generative AI performance report shows impressions for links to your site in AI Overviews and AI Mode, by page, country, date and device. Its documentation describes link impressions, not brand mentions in the answer text. Bing Webmaster Tools reports how often your pages are cited and your share of citations (June 2026).

So the tools show where your pages appeared as links. They do not show whether the answer named your brand. For that, you need the tally.

Is there a standard for measuring mentions and citations?

Yes. The IAB's Measuring Visibility in the AI Era (August 2026) lists mention rate and citation rate as separate measures under “presence”. It defines mention rate as how often a brand appears in AI responses across a defined query set, and citation rate as how often it is cited as a source.

The IAB also says “single-response measurement is not measurement”, asks for results as a range, and separates directional data, which shows trends, from decision-grade data, which can support budget decisions. It leaves specific variability thresholds as an open question.

How do I check mentions and citations myself?

Run a fixed tally: 20 buyer prompts, asked 5 times each, in 2 engines, which gives 200 runs. Record each run in the sheet and recheck at 30 and 90 days. That is a set we use as a working method, not a standard. Increase the numbers if your category is crowded.

  1. Write 20 prompts in the words buyers use, with a mix of comparison, how-to and informational questions.
  2. Ask each prompt 5 times in each engine. Answers change between runs.
  3. Record for every run: whether your brand was mentioned, whether one of your pages was cited, which other brands were mentioned, and which URLs were cited.
  4. Log runs that returned no answer separately. Do not count them as “not mentioned”.
  5. Save the answer text and compare exact cited URLs, not only domains.
  6. Report each result as a share of runs, per engine, and as a range. A single yes or no hides the spread.
  7. Repeat at 30 and 90 days, with the same prompts.

Why will a tracking tool and the ChatGPT app show different results? Tools usually query engines through an API, and the authors of the Toronto preprint warn that API models need not behave like the consumer app. Prompt wording, country and the time of the run also change the answer. Compare like with like, and note which one you used.

Why AI visibility changes across repeated answers

There is another pattern worth watching: brands that are both mentioned and cited may have a stronger chance of appearing again across repeated answers than brands that are only mentioned or only cited. However, this should be treated as an observed association, not a guarantee of repeat visibility. Citation sources can also change over time.

A domain cited today may not be cited in the next month's results, even when the prompt remains the same. This is why AI visibility should be measured across multiple runs and reviewed regularly, rather than judged from a single answer.

Questions

What is a ghost citation?

A ghost citation is when an AI engine cites your page as a source but does not mention your brand in the answer text. Semrush uses the term. In its June 2026 study of 3,981 domain appearances, 61.7% were ghost citations. The page did the work, and the credit went elsewhere or nowhere.

Which AI engine mentions brands most?

In the Semrush study, Gemini mentioned the brand in 83.7% of appearances, the highest of the four engines. Google AI Mode mentioned brands at nearly twice ChatGPT's rate. ChatGPT mentioned the brand in 20.7% of appearances but cited sources in 87%. Results vary by prompt, so test the engines your buyers use.

How do I get mentioned, not just cited?

Get your brand named consistently by sources outside your own site: independent coverage, review sites, community threads and honest inclusion in “best X” lists. Use one brand name everywhere. Then put your name next to every fact on your own pages, so a citation carries the credit with it.

Do AI citations drive traffic?

Some, but the share is small. SE Ranking found AI platforms sent 0.32% of site traffic in January to April 2026, and a Conductor study found 1.08% in 2025. Mentions can also lead buyers to search for you later, and that does not appear as an AI referral.

Does Google Search Console show AI citations?

Partly. Google's generative AI performance report shows impressions for links to your site in AI Overviews and AI Mode, by page, country, date and device. It does not report whether an answer named your brand in its text. For mentions, you need your own tally of runs across engines.

Can an AI engine use my page without linking to it?

Yes. OpenAI's documentation says the number of sources an engine consults is often greater than the number of citations it shows. Ahrefs found only about half of the URLs ChatGPT retrieved, 49.98% across 1.4 million prompts, were cited in the answer.

Which matters more, being mentioned or being cited?

Both matter, for different reasons. A mention puts your name in the buyer's shortlist, and comparison questions draw the most mentions. A citation gives the buyer a page to check and sometimes a click. Which to chase first depends on your state, so track the two separately.

What is generative engine optimization?

Generative engine optimization, or GEO, is the work of making a brand mentioned and its pages cited in answers from AI search engines and large language models (LLMs) such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. It measures mentions and citations separately, by engine, and fixes the gaps.
