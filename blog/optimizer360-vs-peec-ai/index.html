Peec AI vs Optimizer360 AI: Which One Tracks and Fixes Your AI Visibility?

Peec AI vs Optimizer360 AI: Peec AI tracks 3 AI engines on standard plans. Optimizer360 AI tracks 8, runs up to 10,000 prompts a day and ships the fixes.

25 Nov 202611 min readBy the Optimizer360 team

The short answer

Peec AI vs Optimizer360 AI comes down to one question: who does the work? Peec AI is self-serve software that shows how AI answers mention your brand, and your team then acts on that data. A managed GEO programme measures the same things and also writes, ships and re-measures the fixes. Without an expert GEO team, the managed route usually wins.

What is the core difference between Optimizer360 AI and Peec AI?

Peec AI is a dashboard your team operates. Optimizer360 AI is a platform you can sign up to and log in to, plus a team that operates it for you.

Generative engine optimisation (GEO) is the practice of improving how often and how accurately a brand appears in answers from AI systems. It matters because AI answers are replacing clicks. In Pew Research Center's study, Google visits with an AI summary led to a click on a result 8% of the time, against 15% without one.

Peec AI is an AI search analytics tool for marketing teams. It reports visibility, position, sentiment and cited sources, and leaves your team to act on them.

Optimizer360 AI runs on its own platform, Optimizer GEO. The platform scans the engines and ranks the gaps. Then six specialist teams cover brand strategy, GEO strategy, technical, content, digital PR and programme management. They ship the fixes and re-measure them.

Which AI engines does each one track, and what does matching them cost?

Optimizer360 AI measures 8 engines in every programme. Peec AI's standard plans track 3 models that you choose, and each extra model costs more.

Optimizer360 AI measures ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Grok, Perplexity, Kimi, Microsoft Copilot and Google AI Overviews, each scored separately (platform page).

On Peec AI's pricing page, Starter, Pro and Advanced each let you "choose 3" models. You choose from ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overview, AI Mode, Gemini, Naver AI Briefing, ERNIE and Bing AI Mode. Six of those can be added as extra models, at $30, $70 or $140 a month per model depending on the plan. Claude and Grok appear only in Enterprise's list of API models, and the page doesn't list Microsoft Copilot or Kimi.

How many prompts get tracked?

Peec AI plans include 50, 150 or 350 prompts, and each prompt runs once every 24 hours on each selected model. Optimizer GEO runs up to 10,000 prompts per brand per day. The prompts are ranked using SEO data, customer enquiries, a category map and a live engine baseline. So you can map the whole set of questions buyers ask, not just a short list of them.

Who actually ships the fixes?

With Peec AI, your team ships every fix. Optimizer360 AI's team ships them for you, and you approve.

Peec AI's Actions page says it gives a prioritised list of opportunities and can draft briefs and page edits. It also says plainly that "you decide what gets published," and that your team verifies the facts.

What results can a managed GEO programme show?

Optimizer360 AI reports on five KPIs every week, measured against a Week-1 baseline.

The KPIs are visibility score, citation rate, share of voice, sentiment and prompts ranked. In one programme for a residential developer in India, the visibility score rose from 32% to 58% across 8 engines in under 2.5 months. The brand now appears for 80 to 90 tracked buyer questions (see the real-estate AI visibility results). As the FAQ puts it, "That is one programme, not a promise."

The research supports doing the work, not just watching the numbers. The original GEO study, accepted at KDD 2024, found that GEO methods can boost visibility by up to 40% in generative engine responses.

Why does regulated-category sign-off matter?

In regulated categories, a wrong claim in an AI-citable page can become a compliance problem. Optimizer360 AI checks every change before it goes live.

All work passes brand, legal and regulatory sign-off, including RERA, ASCI and SEBI checks where they apply (GEO service page). Optimizer360 AI also commits to no fake reviews, no fake customer posts and no promotional Wikipedia edits. A self-serve tool leaves that review to you.

When is Peec AI the better choice?

Peec AI is a good fit when you already have an SEO team that handles GEO and AI search well and only needs a tracker.

If your SEO team already writes answer-first content, fixes schema, runs digital PR and corrects sources, Peec AI gives them clean daily data. You also get unlimited seats, Looker Studio, an API and an MCP server, at a public price. Users rate it 4.7 out of 5 from 21 reviews on G2 (as of November 2026).

If you don't have an expert GEO team, Peec AI isn't the best fit. It will show you the gaps, and the gaps will stay open. Before you choose either route, read the questions to ask before hiring a GEO firm.

Can you use Peec AI and Optimizer360 AI together?

You can, but you won't need to. Optimizer360 AI comes with its own platform, Optimizer GEO.

Optimizer GEO already covers answer capture, search tracking, a competitor heatmap, source tracking, drift alerts and weekly reports against the baseline. Verdict: which should you choose?

For most brands, choose Optimizer360 AI. You get 8 engines, up to 10,000 prompts a day, and teams that ship and prove the fixes. Choose Peec AI only if you already have an expert GEO team and want a self-serve tracker at a published price.

Questions

Does Peec AI fix AI visibility or only track it?

Peec AI mainly tracks and recommends. Its Actions feature ranks opportunities and can draft briefs or page edits, but its own page says "you decide what gets published." Your team still has to edit, publish, fix schema, run digital PR and correct sources. Optimizer360 AI's specialist teams carry out that work and re-measure each fix against a Week-1 baseline.

Is Optimizer360 AI a tool or a service?

Both. Optimizer GEO is the platform. It scans 8 AI engines, tracks sources and competitors, and stores every answer with its date. Six specialist teams use it to plan, ship and verify fixes across pages, schema, reviews, PR and community answers. Clients can also sign up to Optimizer GEO themselves and get their own access to the platform.

Can anyone guarantee citations in ChatGPT?

No. AI engines decide what they say, so no honest provider can guarantee a ranking, a fixed position in ChatGPT, or a set number of mentions. Optimizer360 AI commits to doing the work rigorously, logging every fix by date and showing weekly what has moved against the Week-1 baseline. Ask any provider that promises guaranteed AI rankings how they could possibly deliver it.

How fast does managed GEO show results?

It depends on your category, your starting point and the sources engines already trust. In one Optimizer360 AI programme, an Indian residential developer's visibility score rose from 32% to 58% across 8 engines in under 2.5 months. That's one programme, not a promise. Weekly reports against the baseline show progress from the first month.

Can I start small with Optimizer360 AI?

Yes. You can start with a Week-1 baseline, which gives you a scorecard, a ranked question map and a costed plan. You can also start with the questions where you already lead. Or run a one-quarter pilot with monthly reporting and a go or stop decision at month 3. None of these needs a long contract.
