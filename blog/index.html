Questions

What is GEO in simple terms?

GEO, or generative engine optimisation, is the work of making sure AI engines such as ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews name your brand, describe it correctly and cite a source for it. It relies on clear facts on your own pages and honest mentions in the sources those engines trust.

Who is this blog written for?

Marketing heads, founders and investor relations teams, mostly in India, who want to understand how AI answers affect their brand. The guides favour practical steps over theory.

Where do the statistics in these guides come from?

Each figure is attributed in the text to its source and year, such as Pew Research Center, Profound, Semrush, Peec AI, Ahrefs and G2. Forecasts are labelled as forecasts. Our own results are described without naming clients.
