What is GEO?

Generative engine optimisation is the work of getting your brand named, cited and described accurately in AI answers from engines like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity.

GEO vs SEO

SEO gets your pages ranked in a list of links. GEO gets your brand named inside the answer. SEO is still the foundation; GEO is the layer buyers now see first.

How AI picks brands

AI engines answer from sources they trust: your site, reviews, press, forums, marketplaces and reference sites. Being cited by those sources is how you get named.

Questions.

What does Optimizer360 do?

Optimizer360 is an AI-native organic marketing company. We run GEO, reputation, feeder networks, content and community, AI agents and lead generation as one engine, so your brand is found, trusted, followed, talked about and chosen without renting reach.

What is GEO, and how is it different from SEO?

SEO ranks your pages in a list of links. GEO (generative engine optimisation) gets your brand named and cited inside AI answers. Most buyers now see the AI answer first, so GEO builds on SEO rather than replacing it.

Which AI engines do you track?
