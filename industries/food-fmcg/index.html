Industries · Food & FMCG

On the list, in the cart.

Families ask AI which snack is healthier, which masala chefs use and whether a ghee is pure. Distributors ask how to stock you. The answer they get decides what goes in the cart.

Get your AI audit →Questions, answered
The short answer

GEO for food and FMCG brands is the work of getting your products named, and described with label-accurate facts, when consumers and distributors ask AI. Engines draw on quick-commerce and marketplace listings, recipe sites, YouTube, food creators and news. Optimizer360 measures 8 engines, fixes the sources they cite and keeps every health or nutrition claim inside FSSAI rules.

What do consumers and distributors ask AI?

Everyday questions with a brand decision inside them.

  • "Healthiest packaged breakfast cereal for kids in India?"

    A health-led shortlist where label facts and claims matter.

  • "Best high-protein snacks without palm oil?"

    An ingredient question. Engines read nutrition panels, reviews and creator videos.

  • "Which masala brand is best for biryani?"

    A recipe-led question answered from food blogs and YouTube cooks.

  • "Is this ghee pure? Any adulteration reports?"

    A trust check that can surface one old news story for years.

  • "Is this product Jain-friendly or vegan?"

    A dietary question that needs exact ingredient data.

  • "How do I become a distributor for this brand in Gujarat?"

    A trade enquiry that should reach your sales team, not a dead form.

Which sources do AI engines lean on for food brands?

  • Quick-commerce and marketplace listings on Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart, BigBasket, Amazon and similar, with their reviews.
  • Recipe sites and food blogs, where brands are named as ingredients.
  • YouTube cooks and Instagram food creators, especially with transcripts and captions.
  • Label-reading creators and nutrition threads on Reddit.
  • News on recalls, quality tests and regulator actions.
  • Your own product pages, when they carry ingredients, nutrition and dietary facts in text.
  • Trade press and directories for distributor and retailer questions.

What usually goes wrong in AI answers for FMCG?

  • Nutrition facts or ingredients quoted from an old pack.
  • A one-off quality story from years ago leading the trust answer.
  • Health claims the engine invents, such as "sugar-free" or "boosts immunity", that your label does not make and you could not make.
  • The brand missing from recipe answers where it is a natural fit.
  • Regional availability wrong, or a product shown as discontinued.
  • Distributor questions answered with an old contact.

What do we do for food and FMCG brands?

  • Brand story and GEO: product pages with label-accurate ingredients, nutrition, veg marks and allergens in plain text, with product and FAQ schema.
  • Reputation: real reviews, public replies and fast factual corrections when an old story resurfaces.
  • Feeder network: a recipe and guide site, a YouTube channel with full transcripts, and disclosed work with food creators.
  • Content and community: open, brand-labelled answers in food and nutrition threads, and digital PR.
  • AI agents: a WhatsApp agent for consumer questions and distributor enquiries.
  • Leads and pipeline: distributor and retailer leads traced back to where they began.

Which rules do we work inside for food brands?

  • FSSAI advertising and claims rules: nutrition and health claims only as permitted and as they appear on the label, care with words such as "natural", "fresh" and "pure", and no disease claims.
  • ASCI: evidence for every claim, disclosed creator posts and extra care with food advertising aimed at children.
  • Packaging rules: the facts we publish match the declared pack information.
  • DPDP Act: consent for WhatsApp and email marketing.

Questions

How do I get my snack brand mentioned when people ask ChatGPT for healthy snacks?

Publish clear, label-accurate nutrition and ingredient facts, build real reviews where shoppers buy, and earn mentions from the creators and guides the engines cite. We track those questions daily and fix the sources behind them, without overstating any health benefit.

Can AI repeat a health claim we are not allowed to make?

Yes, engines sometimes attach claims from forums or creators to a product. We watch for this, publish the correct label facts in a form the engines can quote and report whether the answer changes.

Do quick-commerce listings affect AI answers?

They can, along with marketplace reviews and recipe content. Our Sources view shows which listings and pages are cited in your category, so effort goes where it counts.

Can GEO help us find distributors?

It can make sure distributor questions about your brand get an accurate answer and a working route to your sales team. We then trace each enquiry back to where it started.
