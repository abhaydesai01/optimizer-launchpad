Industries · Pharma & healthcare

Right facts, right care.

Patients and caregivers ask AI which hospital to trust, which lab to book and what a medicine does. In healthcare a wrong answer is more than a lost lead.

Get your AI audit →Questions, answered
The short answer

GEO for pharma and healthcare is the work of making AI answers about a hospital, clinic, lab or medicine accurate, current and properly sourced. Engines lean on medical references, regulators, journals, doctor listings and reviews. Optimizer360 measures 8 engines and fixes those sources with clinically reviewed content, making no medical claims beyond approved labelling and using no patient testimonials.

What do patients and caregivers ask AI?

Questions where accuracy matters more than reach.

  • "Best hospital for knee replacement in Chennai?"

    A shortlist that leans on accreditation, doctor profiles, reviews and news.

  • "Which diagnostic lab near Andheri offers home sample collection?"

    A local service question answered from listings and Google Business Profiles.

  • "Which cardiologist at this hospital treats arrhythmia?"

    A doctor-level question that old staff lists often get wrong.

  • "Does this hospital offer cashless treatment with my insurer?"

    A practical check that sends patients elsewhere when the answer is vague.

  • "What are the side effects of this medicine?"

    A medical question that must rest on approved information, not forum posts.

Which sources do AI engines lean on in healthcare?

  • Medical references and drug information databases.
  • Regulators and approved product information, including CDSCO and approved prescribing information.
  • Journals and indexed studies.
  • Hospital and doctor profiles on your site and on doctor-listing platforms.
  • Accreditation listings such as NABH and NABL.
  • Google reviews and health news.
  • Wikipedia, patient forums and Reddit, which engines sometimes blend into medical facts.

What usually goes wrong in AI answers for healthcare?

  • Doctor lists, departments or timings out of date.
  • Accreditation or insurance tie-ups stated wrongly.
  • A medicine described with benefits beyond its approved label, or brand and generic names mixed up.
  • A single complaint or news story leading the answer about a hospital.
  • Patient anecdotes from forums repeated as if they were medical fact.

What do we do for hospitals, clinics and pharma brands?

  • Brand story and GEO: department, doctor and service pages with accreditation facts, clinically reviewed FAQs and medical organisation schema.
  • Reputation: public replies to reviews that never disclose patient information, and fast correction of wrong facts.
  • Feeder network: patient education guides and video explainers with transcripts, reviewed by your clinicians.
  • Content and community: general health information in open, brand-labelled answers, never diagnosis.
  • AI agents: agents that book appointments and answer timings and insurance questions, never give medical advice and route urgent cases to your staff.

Which rules do we work inside in healthcare?

  • Approved labelling only: no medical claims beyond what the regulator has approved, and no promotion of prescription medicines to the public.
  • Drugs and Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act: no claims to cure or treat the conditions it covers.
  • ASCI healthcare guidelines: evidence for every claim and no misleading comparisons.
  • No patient testimonials and no before-and-after stories.
  • Medical council rules on how doctors present themselves, and the pharmaceutical marketing code for content aimed at doctors.
  • DPDP Act: health information treated as sensitive, with consent for every enquiry.

Clinical, legal and regulatory sign-off comes before anything goes live.

Questions

Can a hospital do GEO without breaking advertising rules?

Yes. GEO in healthcare is mostly about accuracy: correct doctor, department, accreditation and insurance facts, and clear patient information. We make no claims beyond approved labelling, use no testimonials and get clinical and legal sign-off first.

How do I correct wrong information ChatGPT gives about our medicine?

We find the sources behind the answer, then publish approved product information in a form the engines can quote and correct third-party pages where we can. We re-measure weekly and report whether the answer has changed.

Will you use patient testimonials or success stories?

No. We do not use patient testimonials or before-and-after stories. We reply to reviews in public without disclosing any patient information.

Can an AI chat agent book appointments for our hospital?

Yes. It can book appointments and answer timings, departments and insurance questions on WhatsApp and the website. It never gives medical advice and routes urgent cases to your team.
