Industries · Retail & consumer brands

Be the brand AI suggests.

Shoppers now ask AI what to buy, then check the reviews. If your products are missing from the answer, or described wrongly, the sale goes to someone else.

Get your AI audit →Questions, answered
The short answer

GEO for retail and D2C brands is the work of getting your products named, and described correctly, when shoppers ask AI what to buy. Engines draw on marketplace listings and reviews, creator videos, "best X in India" lists and Reddit. Optimizer360 measures 8 engines, fixes the product facts and review trail they cite, and keeps claims inside consumer protection and ASCI rules.

What do shoppers ask AI?

Questions we see in consumer categories, from first idea to checkout.

  • "Best sunscreen for oily skin in an Indian summer?"

    A category question. Two or three brands get named and the rest are invisible.

  • "Is this brand better than that one for running shoes?"

    A comparison the engine settles with reviews, creator videos and spec pages.

  • "Best Indian mattress brand with a long trial?"

    A D2C shortlist built from listicles, review threads and brand pages.

  • "Is this brand legit? How are delivery and returns?"

    A trust check that pulls from marketplace reviews and complaint threads.

  • "Sustainable ethnic wear brands made in India?"

    A values question. Engines look for evidence, not slogans.

  • "Where can I buy this brand offline in Hyderabad?"

    A location question answered from store pages and Google Business Profiles.

Which sources do AI engines lean on for consumer brands?

  • Marketplace listings and reviews on Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Nykaa and similar platforms.
  • Your own product pages, when they carry clear specs, ingredients, sizes and FAQs.
  • YouTube reviews and Instagram creators, especially videos with transcripts.
  • "Best X in India" lists and comparison blogs.
  • Reddit communities on skincare, fitness, fashion and home.
  • Google Business Profiles and reviews for physical stores.
  • Startup and business media covering launches and funding.

What usually goes wrong in AI answers for retail brands?

  • Incumbents named by default because the lists the engines quote are years old.
  • Wrong ingredients, sizes or specs copied from reseller listings.
  • Discontinued products recommended while new ones are missing.
  • Old delivery or returns complaints still shaping the trust answer.
  • The brand confused with a similarly named one in another category.
  • Store locations out of date.

What do we do for retail and D2C brands?

  • Brand story and GEO: product fact pages, honest comparison pages and product and FAQ schema, so engines quote you and not a reseller.
  • Reputation: a steady stream of real reviews from real customers, every review answered in public and drift alerts after launches or price changes.
  • Feeder network: buying guides, a video channel, a WhatsApp channel, and earned places with creators and "best X" lists, clearly disclosed.
  • Content and community: a community of real customers and open, brand-labelled answers on Reddit.
  • AI agents: Instagram, WhatsApp and website agents that answer size, ingredient and order questions accurately.
  • Leads and pipeline: orders traced back to the answer, review or video where demand began.

Which rules do we work inside for consumer brands?

  • Consumer Protection Act and CCPA guidelines on misleading advertisements and dark patterns.
  • ASCI code and influencer guidelines: every paid creator post is disclosed, and claims carry evidence.
  • The BIS standard on online consumer reviews: no paid or fake reviews, and no hiding negative ones.
  • DPDP Act: consent before marketing messages on WhatsApp or email.

Questions

How do I get my D2C brand into ChatGPT's product recommendations?

Give the engines evidence they can quote: clear product pages, real reviews across the sites shoppers use, creator coverage and places on credible "best X" lists. We track the shopping questions in your category and fix the sources the engines actually cite.

Do Amazon and Flipkart reviews affect AI answers?

Marketplace pages and reviews often appear among the sources engines cite for product questions. Our Sources view shows exactly which ones matter for your category, so we know where real reviews and accurate listings will count.

Will you write reviews for our products?

No. We help you ask real customers for honest reviews and answer every review in public. Fake or paid reviews break consumer rules and damage trust when they are found.

Is GEO different from marketplace SEO?

Marketplace SEO ranks a listing inside one store. GEO shapes the answer a shopper gets before they open any store, which draws on many sources at once, including your own site, reviews, creators and forums.
