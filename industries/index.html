Why does each industry need a different GEO approach?

Generative engine optimisation (GEO, spelt optimization in the US) works the same way everywhere at the level of method: find the questions, see who the engines name, trace which sources they quote, fix those sources and measure again. What changes by industry is everything inside that method.

  • The questions differ. A homebuyer asks about possession dates. An investor asks about the last quarter. A procurement engineer asks about a material grade.
  • The sources differ. Engines lean on property portals for flats, exchange filings for listed companies, review sites for software and medical references for medicines.
  • The rules differ. RERA, SEBI, FSSAI, the Drugs and Magic Remedies Act, professional body codes and CBFC certification all limit what a brand may say, and so what we may publish for it.

That is why we start every programme with a Week-1 baseline built from your own category, not a template.

Questions

Does GEO work the same way in every industry?

The method is the same: scan, diagnose, fix and prove. The questions, the sources the engines trust and the rules on what you may say are different in each sector. We build the question universe and source map for your category before we change anything.

Which industries does Optimizer360 work in?

We work in ten: real estate and hospitality, financial services and investor relations, retail and consumer brands, food and FMCG, B2B and professional services, technology and SaaS, pharma and healthcare, manufacturing, industrial and engineering, and cinema and entertainment. Our home market is India and we also work in the United States and Mauritius.

How do you handle regulated industries like finance and healthcare?

Every piece of content goes through brand, legal and regulatory sign-off before it goes live. We use public disclosures only for listed companies, follow SEBI rules, avoid medical claims beyond approved labelling and never publish fabricated reviews or figures.

My industry is not on the list. Can GEO still help?

Usually, yes. Most categories share the same building blocks: accurate fact pages, real reviews, cited press and honest community presence. Write to us and we can run a baseline to see which sources decide your answers.
