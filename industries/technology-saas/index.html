Industries · Technology & SaaS

Be the tool AI names first.

Software buyers ask AI for the best tool, the cheapest alternative and how two products compare. The engines answer from review sites, docs and comparison pages, often written by someone else.

Get your AI audit →Questions, answered
The short answer

GEO for SaaS is the work of getting your product named, and described with current features, when buyers ask AI which tool to use. Engines draw on software review sites, product docs, comparison and alternatives pages, Reddit and tech media. Optimizer360 measures 8 engines, makes your docs and comparisons quotable, builds real reviews and traces demos back to the answer.

What do software buyers ask AI?

Evaluation questions, asked before anyone books a demo.

  • "Best CRM for a 20-person sales team in India with WhatsApp integration?"

    A shortlist with requirements. Missing one integration in the answer drops you out.

  • "This tool vs that tool for payroll in India?"

    A comparison often settled by whichever side wrote the clearer page.

  • "Alternatives to a global tool for Indian startups?"

    An alternatives query where review sites and listicles dominate.

  • "Does this product integrate with Tally?"

    A feature check answered from docs, if the engine can read them.

  • "Is customer data stored in India?"

    A security and compliance question that needs a current, exact answer.

  • "How do I set up approval workflows in this product?"

    A how-to that keeps users, or sends them to a competitor's tutorial.

Which sources do AI engines lean on for SaaS?

  • Software review sites such as G2 and Capterra, and their category grids.
  • Product docs, help centres and changelogs, when they are public and crawlable.
  • Comparison and alternatives pages, both yours and your competitors'.
  • Reddit communities for SaaS, startups and developers.
  • GitHub and Stack Overflow for developer tools.
  • YouTube tutorials and reviews with transcripts.
  • Tech media and launch coverage.

What usually goes wrong in AI answers for SaaS?

  • Features, plans or integrations quoted from an old version.
  • A competitor's comparison page shaping how your product is described.
  • The product placed in the wrong category, so it never appears in the right shortlist.
  • Docs behind a login or rendered only in JavaScript, so engines cite a third-party tutorial instead.
  • A thin review site profile that makes a strong product look unproven.
  • Wrong answers on data residency, security or compliance.

What do we do for SaaS and technology companies?

  • Brand story and GEO: our Brand and Product package for vendor shortlists, with fair comparison pages, crawlable docs, a public changelog and software schema.
  • Reputation: a steady programme of real user reviews on the sites buyers check, every review answered and drift alerts after releases.
  • Feeder network: a blog, newsletter and tutorial channel, plus earned places on credible "best X" lists.
  • Content and community: a user community on your own site and open, brand-labelled answers on Reddit.
  • AI agents: a website agent that answers product and security questions accurately and books demos.
  • Leads and pipeline: trials and demos traced back to the answer, review or thread where they began.

Which rules do we work inside for software companies?

  • ASCI on comparisons: comparative claims must be fair, factual and verifiable, and we treat competitors' names and marks with care.
  • Review site rules: incentives for reviews are disclosed and no review is written for a user.
  • DPDP Act: what you say about data handling must match what the product does, and lead data is collected with consent.
  • Security claims: certifications are stated only while they are current.

Questions

How do I get my SaaS product recommended by ChatGPT?

Make sure the engines can read accurate, current facts about your product, see real reviews from real users and find you in the comparisons and lists buyers trust. We track the shortlist questions in your category and fix the sources the engines cite for them.

Do comparison pages help in AI answers?

Fair, factual comparison pages are often quoted, which is why a competitor's page can end up describing your product. We publish honest comparisons with verifiable facts, inside ASCI's rules on comparative claims.

Should our product docs be public for AI engines?

Usually the parts that answer buyer questions should be. If docs sit behind a login or only render in JavaScript, engines tend to quote someone else's tutorial instead.

Can you get us more reviews on G2 or Capterra?

We run a steady programme of asking real users for honest reviews and answering them in public. We never write, buy or fake reviews.

Does GEO replace SEO for SaaS?

No. Strong SEO pages often become the sources AI engines quote. We report on both scoreboards: the AI answers, and SEO plus owned enquiries.
