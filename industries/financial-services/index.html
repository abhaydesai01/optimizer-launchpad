Industries · Financial services & investor relations

Accurate answers investors can trust.

Customers ask AI which lender is safe. Investors ask AI what happened last quarter. In both cases a stale or wrong answer costs trust you cannot buy back with ads.

The short answer

GEO for financial services is the work of making AI answers about a lender, insurer, fintech or listed company accurate, current and properly sourced. Engines draw on exchange filings, regulator registers, financial news, Wikipedia and Reddit. Optimizer360 measures 8 engines and fixes those sources using public disclosures only, following SEBI rules, with company sign-off before anything is published.

What do customers and investors ask AI?

Two audiences, one problem: the answer is often out of date.

  • "Is this NBFC safe for a fixed deposit?"

    A trust question. The answer leans on credit ratings, regulator status and news.

  • "Best personal loan app in India with no hidden charges?"

    A shortlist question shaped by comparison sites, app reviews and Reddit.

  • "Is this fintech app registered with RBI?"

    A regulatory check. A vague answer here sends users elsewhere.

  • "Which health insurer settles claims fastest?"

    Engines reach for published claim data and review threads.

  • "What were this company's results last quarter?"

    Investors expect the latest filing. Engines often quote an older one.

  • "Why did this company's shares fall?"

    The answer can lead with a controversy that was resolved long ago.

Which sources do AI engines lean on in financial services?

  • Stock exchange filings, annual reports and investor presentations for listed companies.
  • Regulator websites and registers: SEBI, RBI and IRDAI lists of registered entities and published data.
  • Financial news and business media, which often outrank your own investor page.
  • Wikipedia, the single largest share of ChatGPT's top cited sources.
  • Comparison sites and app store reviews for loans, cards, insurance and investing apps.
  • Reddit personal finance communities and YouTube finance creators, whose opinions are sometimes repeated as fact.

What usually goes wrong in AI answers for financial brands?

  • Old quarterly figures quoted as current after new results are filed.
  • A resolved controversy or past regulatory action still leading the answer.
  • The company confused with a group entity or a similarly named firm.
  • Rates, fees or product features quoted from old pages.
  • A creator's opinion presented as the company's own position.
  • The brand missing from "best X" shortlists because comparison sites carry stale data.

What do we do for financial services and listed companies?

  • GEO for investor relations: for listed companies, we keep what AI tells investors accurate and current, using only public disclosures. Accuracy, not spin.
  • Brand story and GEO: product and company fact pages, FAQs and schema that engines can quote, refreshed after every rate change or filing.
  • Reputation: public replies to reviews, factual corrections and drift alerts after results or announcements.
  • Content and community: plain explainers and open, brand-labelled answers in finance threads, without advice.
  • AI agents: chat agents that answer product questions from approved content and hand anything personal to licensed staff.

In one investor relations programme for a listed company in India, the AI answer to investor questions moved from old figures and a resolved controversy to the latest filing. See how the narrative changed.

Which rules do we work inside in financial services?

  • SEBI: public disclosures only, nothing unpublished or price-sensitive, nothing ahead of an exchange filing, and no forward-looking promises.
  • Who may advise: we stay inside SEBI's limits on investment advice and on tie-ups with unregistered finance creators.
  • RBI and IRDAI expectations for regulated lenders and insurers, including required disclosures and risk statements.
  • ASCI: no assured returns and no claims without evidence.
  • DPDP Act: lead and chat data handled with consent.

The company signs off every change before it is published.

Questions

How do I correct what ChatGPT says about my listed company?

Find the sources it is quoting, then make the current, public facts easier to cite than the old ones. We map every citation, publish accurate pages from your filings, correct Wikipedia through the talk page with sources and re-measure weekly.

Is GEO for investor relations allowed under SEBI rules?

It is built around them. We use only information you have already disclosed publicly, never share anything unpublished or price-sensitive, and nothing goes live without company sign-off.

Can you make AI answers say our stock is a good investment?

No. That would be advice and spin. Our job is accuracy: the latest figures, the right context and current sources.

Why does AI still mention an old controversy about our company?

Older news and forum threads have had years to be cited, while the resolution may sit in a single filing. We find where the old story is coming from and make the resolved, current position easy for the engines to find and quote.

Can an AI chat agent answer customer questions about our loans or policies?

Yes, from approved content only. It answers product questions, never gives personalised financial advice and hands anything personal to your licensed team.
