- **Brand sentiment:** the tone of how engines describe the brand, scored on every stored answer.

## Research behind the playbook
- Reddit and Wikipedia are among the most-cited sources in AI answers; entity authority (a maintained Wikipedia page) correlates strongly with brand mention rates (vendor studies, directional).
- The Princeton GEO study found adding statistics and citing authoritative sources raised visibility in generative answers.
- IR guidance (Q4, Euronext, TSX): make IR materials machine-readable, or AI fills gaps with weaker sources; the issuer stays responsible for accuracy.
