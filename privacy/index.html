Privacy

Privacy, kept simple.

Last updated: 10 November 2026. What this website collects, what it does not, and how to reach us about your data.

The short answer

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. It stores one setting, your colour-mode preference, in your browser's localStorage. The contact and audit forms do not send data to a server: they open your own email app with a prefilled message to abhay@optimizer360.ai. We use the emails we receive only to reply, never sell them, and keep them only as long as needed.

Who does this notice cover?

This notice covers the website optimizer360.ai, run by Optimizer360. Where we receive personal data from you, we decide how it is used, which under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 makes us the data fiduciary.

Last updated: 10 November 2026.

What does this website collect?

  • No analytics or advertising cookies. We do not use them.
  • Your colour-mode preference. If you switch between light and dark mode, the choice is saved in your browser's localStorage so the site remembers it. It stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.
  • Standard technical data. Like any website, the servers that deliver these pages may record basic technical information, such as IP address, browser type and the page requested, to keep the site secure and running. We do not use it to profile visitors.

How do the contact and audit forms work?

The forms on this site do not submit anything to a server. When you press the button, your own email app opens with a message to abhay@optimizer360.ai, filled in with what you typed. Nothing reaches us unless you choose to send that email. Your email provider handles the message under its own privacy terms.

What happens to emails you send us?

  • We use them to reply to you and to carry on the conversation you started.
  • We do not sell them, and we do not use them for advertising.
  • We keep them only as long as we need them for that conversation and any work that follows, or as long as the law requires.

What are your rights under India's DPDP Act 2023?

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 you have the right to:

  • Access: ask what personal data we hold about you and how we use it.
  • Correction: ask us to correct, complete or update it.
  • Erasure: ask us to delete it, unless the law requires us to keep it.
  • Grievance redressal: raise a complaint with us. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Nomination: name someone to exercise these rights for you in the event of death or incapacity.

To use any of these rights, email abhay@optimizer360.ai.

Visitors from the EU, UK and US

EU and UK: if the GDPR or UK GDPR applies to you, you have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, object to and move your personal data, and to complain to your local data protection authority. We process the emails you send us to reply to your request.

United States: we do not sell or share your personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising. You can ask us to tell you what we hold or to delete it.

How do I contact you about privacy?

Email abhay@optimizer360.ai with "Privacy" in the subject line.

If we add analytics or a form backend, this notice will be updated first.

No. It opens your own email app with a prefilled message to abhay@optimizer360.ai. Nothing reaches us unless you choose to send that email.
