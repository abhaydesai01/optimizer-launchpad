About

Stop renting growth. Own it.

We make brands found, trusted, followed, talked about and chosen in AI answers, search, media and communities.

Get your AI audit →Questions, answered
The short answer

Optimizer360 is an AI-native organic marketing company based in India, also working in the United States and Mauritius. We make brands found, trusted, followed, talked about and chosen in AI answers, search, media and communities. AI agents find the gaps, expert teams fix them, and data proves what moved, measured every week against a Week-1 baseline.

Why do we exist?

Most growth is rented. Ads, sponsored posts and paid placements stop working the day the budget stops. Owned growth is different: the pages you write and keep true, the reviews and press buyers trust, the community that recommends you, the channels you build. Those keep working, and they compound.

Buyers now ask AI engines before they ask you. What those engines say comes from what is published about you, by you and by others. We exist to help brands own that growth, and to prove it with data.

What does AI-native mean here?

AI agents and expert teams, run as one organic engine.

  • Agents find it

    Our platform tracks 8 AI engines and up to 10,000 prompts per brand a day, recording who is named, in what order and quoting which source.

  • Experts fix it

    Specialist teams ship the pages, schema, review replies, community answers and PR, and log every fix with its date.

  • Data proves it

    Every change is re-measured and reported weekly against the Week-1 baseline, on the AI answers and on SEO and owned enquiries.

Who founded Optimizer360?

Salil Chaturvedi, founder

A serial entrepreneur, investor and mentor. Salil founded Provogue and Prozone. He is a YPO member and the Founding President of EO Navi Mumbai.

Abhay Desai, founding team

Abhay leads technology and product, with experience across fintech, edtech and other sectors. He built the Optimizer360 platform and runs delivery.

Which teams do the work?

Six specialist teams own the KPIs and review them weekly with your CMO.

  • Brand strategy

    Defines the story and the facts the brand should be known for.

  • GEO strategy

    Builds the prompt universe, reads the answers and sets the priorities.

  • Technical

    Schema, site structure and the readiness work that lets engines read you.

  • Content

    Answer-first pages, FAQs, guides and community answers.

  • Digital PR

    Real press, lists and coverage in the media engines trust.

  • Programme management

    Keeps the plan, the sign-offs and the weekly reporting on track.

What are our principles?

  • Accuracy first

    We make AI answers true, current and sourced. No fabricated reviews or figures.

  • No fake reviews

    Real reviews from real customers, and a public reply to every one. No astroturfing.

  • Open in communities

    On Reddit and Quora we post openly as the brand. On Wikipedia we work only through talk pages, with sources.

  • We never name clients

    No client names, logos, counts, quotes or testimonials, anywhere.

  • No ranking promises

    The engines decide. We do the work and show every week what moved.

  • Sign-off before go-live

    Brand, legal and regulatory sign-off, with RERA, ASCI and SEBI checks, before anything ships.

Where do we work?

Our home market is India. We also work in the United States and Mauritius.

We work across 10 industries: real estate and hospitality; financial services and investor relations; retail and consumer brands; food and FMCG; B2B and professional services; technology and SaaS; pharma and healthcare; manufacturing; industrial and engineering; cinema and entertainment.
