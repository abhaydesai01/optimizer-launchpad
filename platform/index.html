The short answer

Optimizer GEO is the platform Optimizer360 built to track how brands appear in AI answers. It runs up to 1,000 prompts per brand a day across eight engines, including ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews, recording who is named, in what order and which sources are cited. Our teams use it to plan, ship and prove every fix.

Questions

How can I track what ChatGPT says about my brand?

Ask it the questions your buyers ask, many times and on a schedule, and keep every answer with its date and sources. Doing that by hand does not scale, which is why Optimizer GEO runs up to 1,000 prompts per brand a day across eight engines.

Which AI engines does Optimizer GEO track?

Eight: ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Grok, Perplexity, Kimi, Microsoft Copilot and Google AI Overviews. Each is measured separately because each favours different sources.

Is there search volume for AI prompts?

No. AI engines do not publish how often a question is asked, and we do not make up a number. We rank questions using your SEO data, your own enquiries, a category map and a live engine baseline.

Can I sign up for Optimizer GEO on my own?

Not at present. The platform is used inside our programmes, where our teams act on what it finds. A GEO baseline is the quickest way to see your own scorecard, prompt universe and live answers.

How often is the data updated?

Engines are scanned daily, and every fix is logged with its date. You receive a weekly report that compares each measure with your Week-1 baseline.
