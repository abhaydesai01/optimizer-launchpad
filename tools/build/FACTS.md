## The six things you should own (the solutions)
1. **Brand story / GEO** (generative engine optimisation): the facts AI repeats about you come from pages you write and keep true; get named and cited in AI answers.
2. **Reputation**: a steady stream of real reviews and real press that buyers, and AI, both trust. Answer every review in public; correct facts; drift alerts.
3. **Feeder network**: the sites and channels you build (blog, guide site, video channel, newsletter, WhatsApp channel) plus the creators, "best X in Y" lists and newsletters you earn, all sending buyers and AI your way.
4. **Content & community**: real customers who discuss, defend and recommend you, in a room you help keep alive (communities on your own site, Reddit/Quora presence posted openly as the brand), plus content agents and digital PR.
5. **AI agents (conversations)**: 24/7 chat agents on the website, WhatsApp and Instagram that answer accurately, qualify leads and book calls/visits.
6. **Leads & pipeline**: every deal traced back to where demand began (an AI answer, a review, a thread, a video), so you fund what works. Organic attribution from first mention to closed deal.

Rented vs owned framing: rented reach (ads, sponsored posts, paid placements) stops the day the budget stops; owned assets keep working and compound.
