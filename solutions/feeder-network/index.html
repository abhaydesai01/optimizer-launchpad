Solutions · Feeder network

Build the roads in.

Your website is one door. A feeder network adds the guides, channels, lists and creators that lead buyers, and AI engines, to it.

Get your AI audit →Questions, answered
The short answer

A feeder network is the set of channels a brand owns or earns that send buyers, and AI engines, back to it. Owned feeders include a blog, a guide site, a video channel, a newsletter and a WhatsApp channel. Earned feeders include creators, credible "best X in Y" lists and independent newsletters. Together they create the brand mentions that AI engines look for.

What is a feeder network?

Most brands rely on one website and whatever paid reach they can buy. A feeder network spreads your presence across the places buyers already spend time, and where AI engines look for independent signals. Some of those places you build and own. Others you earn by being worth writing about.

Every feeder carries the same verified facts as your main site, so wherever a buyer or an engine meets you, the story is consistent. That consistency is what turns many small mentions into one clear picture.

What do you own?

  • Blog

    Answer-first articles on the questions your buyers ask AI, written to be quoted.

  • Guide site

    A dedicated, clearly branded resource on your category or city, useful in its own right.

  • Video channel

    Walkthroughs and explainers with full transcripts, so the content can be read as text as well as watched.

  • Newsletter

    A regular note to people who asked for it, building an audience you do not rent.

  • WhatsApp channel

    Updates delivered where many Indian buyers already read and share.

What do you earn?

  • Creators

    People your buyers already follow, who cover you because you are relevant, with any partnership disclosed.

  • "Best X in Y" lists

    Credible roundups such as best developers in a city or best tools for a task, the pages engines often cite.

  • Independent newsletters

    Editors in your sector who mention you when you have something worth saying.

  • Trade and business media

    Coverage earned through digital PR, which engines read as an independent source.

Do brand mentions matter more than backlinks for AI visibility?

A large Ahrefs study of 75,000 brands found that brand mentions track AI Overview visibility much more closely than backlinks do. That is a correlation, not proof of cause, but it matches what we see in the sources engines cite: they lean on places that talk about a brand, not only places that link to it.

A feeder network is built to create those mentions honestly, through useful content and earned coverage. Read how AI engines choose brands to cite for more on what they trust.

How do we build a feeder network?

  1. 01

    Map

    We list the sources engines already cite for your buyer questions, using the Sources view of our platform.

  2. 02

    Build

    We set up the owned channels that fill the gaps, starting with the ones your buyers use most.

  3. 03

    Our digital PR team pitches creators, list editors and media with real facts and stories.

  4. 04

  5. 05

Not a link scheme: every owned feeder is clearly yours and useful on its own. We do not build networks of fake independent sites, disguise paid placements as editorial or create sham review pages. Engines and buyers both see through them, and they put the rest of your reputation at risk.

Questions

Is a feeder network the same as a private blog network?

No. A private blog network hides its ownership to manipulate rankings. Our owned feeders are openly branded and built to be useful to readers. Earned feeders are independent creators and publications that choose to mention you.

Do brand mentions help AI visibility more than backlinks?

In one Ahrefs study of 75,000 brands, mentions correlated far more strongly with AI Overview visibility (0.664) than backlinks did (0.218). That shows association, not cause, but it is a good reason to invest in being talked about, not just linked to.

How do we get onto "best X in Y" lists?

By earning it. We find the lists engines cite for your questions, check what the editors value, and pitch them with clear facts, proof and access. Paid placements bought quietly are rented reach and are not part of this work.

Does YouTube help with AI visibility?

It can. Video with a full transcript gives engines text they can read and quote, and a walkthrough answers buyer questions that a page alone cannot. We publish transcripts with every video we produce.

Do we need to build a separate website for a guide site?

Not always. If your main site can host a strong, well-structured guide section, that may be enough. A separate guide site makes sense when the topic is broader than your brand and deserves its own home, and it is always clearly marked as yours.
