Solutions · Content and community

Get talked about.

The most persuasive voice for your brand is a real customer. We help you earn those voices and keep the conversation going, in the open.

Get your AI audit →Questions, answered
The short answer

Community-led growth means real customers discuss, defend and recommend your brand in places you help keep alive: a community on your own site, Reddit and Quora threads where the brand posts openly under its own name, and the media through digital PR. Content agents draft, people edit and approve, and nothing is posted as a fake customer.

Why do communities and forums shape AI answers?

AI engines want to know what real people think, so they read forums. Reddit is now the most directly cited source across the major AI engines, and Quora threads appear often too. If the conversation about your category happens without you, the engine learns your category from someone else.

That does not mean flooding forums. It means showing up where your buyers already ask questions, as yourself, with answers that are genuinely useful.

What does content and community include?

  • A community you host

    A space on your own site where customers ask, answer and share, which you help moderate and keep alive.

  • Reddit and Quora, openly

    Answers in relevant threads from a clearly labelled brand account, following each forum's rules.

  • Content agents

    AI agents that find questions worth answering and draft replies and articles for your team to edit.

  • Answer-first content

    Articles, guides and FAQs that settle a question in the first paragraph, written to be quoted.

  • Digital PR

    Stories pitched to trade and business media, earning coverage that buyers and engines treat as independent.

How can a brand use Reddit without astroturfing?

Reddit marketing for brands works only when it is done openly. Our rules are simple:

  • Post from an account that is clearly the brand, never a fake customer.
  • Answer the question that was asked, with facts, even when the honest answer is not your product.
  • Follow each community's own rules, and stay out where brands are not welcome.
  • Never buy upvotes or coordinate accounts to push a thread.

In one real-estate programme, open answers in city and home-buying threads were part of what lifted the developer's AI visibility. More on the method in how to get cited by AI engines.

How does the content engine run each week?

  1. 01

    Find

    Content agents surface threads, questions and story angles from your prompt universe and category forums.

  2. 02

    Draft

    Agents prepare first drafts using only your verified facts.

  3. 03

    Edit and approve

    Our content team sharpens each piece, and your team signs off anything regulated or sensitive.

  4. 04

    Post openly

    Replies go out as the brand, articles on your owned channels, pitches to named editors.

  5. 05

    Measure

    We track replies, citations by AI engines and enquiries that mention a thread or article.

What does digital PR add to community work?

Communities show what customers think. Press shows what independent observers think. Engines weigh both. Our digital PR team pitches real stories, data and expert comment to trade and business media, and works to place you on credible "best of" lists and local guides.

Good coverage also strengthens your reputation and becomes part of your feeder network, sending readers back to you long after the story runs.

Posted openly, as the brand: no fake customers, no sock-puppet accounts, no paid posts disguised as opinion. If a community would object to knowing who wrote something, we do not post it.

Questions

Is it okay for a brand to post on Reddit?

Yes, if it is honest about who it is and follows each subreddit's rules. Many communities welcome clear, helpful answers from a labelled brand account. They do not welcome disguised promotion, and neither do we.

Will Reddit users just downvote a brand account?

Some will, especially if the reply sounds like an advert. Useful, specific answers that admit limits tend to be received far better. We also stay out of communities where brands are not wanted.

Who writes the content, AI or people?

Both. Content agents find questions and prepare first drafts from your verified facts. Our content team edits every piece, and your team approves anything regulated or sensitive before it is posted.

Should we build our own community or join existing forums?

Usually both. Existing forums are where buyers ask questions today and where engines read. A community on your own site is where loyal customers gather and where you control the space. We help you decide the balance for your category.

What is the difference between digital PR and paid media?

Paid media buys space, and the reader knows it is an advert. Digital PR earns coverage because the story, data or expert view is worth reporting. Engines treat earned coverage as an independent source, which is why it matters for AI visibility.
