The short answer

Generative engine optimisation (GEO) is the work of getting a brand named, cited and described accurately in AI answers from engines such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. A GEO agency measures what those engines say today, finds the sources they trust, fixes the pages, reviews and press behind each answer, then re-measures every week.

What is generative engine optimisation?

Generative engine optimisation (also spelled generative engine optimization) is the practice of shaping what AI engines say when a buyer asks about your category. You will also see it called AI search optimisation, answer engine optimisation (AEO), LLM visibility or, informally, ChatGPT SEO. The goal is the same: be named in the answer, be cited as a source, and be described with facts that are true today.

SEO earns a position in a list of links. GEO earns a sentence inside the answer itself. The two share foundations, such as crawlable pages and clear structure, but GEO also depends on the third-party sources engines read: reviews, forums, Wikipedia, press and comparison lists. Read what generative engine optimisation is for the long version, or GEO vs SEO for a side-by-side view.

Why does GEO matter now?

Fewer searches end on your website. When Google shows an AI summary, people click far less, and almost nobody clicks the links inside it. B2B buyers increasingly start their research in an AI chat tool rather than a search box. If the answer does not name you, many buyers never reach a page where you could make your case.

The good news is that answers can be influenced with honest work. Research from Princeton and IIT Delhi found that content with statistics, quotations and cited sources is more visible in generative answers.

How does GEO work?

  1. 01

    Scan

    We run your buyers' real questions across all eight engines and capture every answer with its date: who is named, in what order, and which sources are quoted.

  2. 02

    Diagnose

    We score you on an 8-factor readiness scorecard, rank the questions by demand and winnability, and find the sources that decide each answer.

  3. 03

    Our teams ship AI-citable pages, FAQs, schema, review replies and PR. Every fix is logged with its date.

  4. 04

    Prove

What does a GEO programme ship?

  • Answer-first pages

    Blogs and buyer guides that answer one question in the first paragraph, with real numbers engines can quote.

  • Fact pages and FAQs

    One page per product, project or service with the specifics buyers ask for, plus FAQ and comparison pages.

  • Schema and entities

    Structured data for your company, products and FAQs, one clean Wikidata entity, and consistent Google Business Profiles and listings.

  • Corrections at the source

    Wrong facts fixed where engines read them. Wikipedia changes go through the talk page with sources, never as promotional edits.

  • Reviews and replies

    Public replies to reviews on Google and the portals in your category. See reputation for the full service.

  • PR, lists and video

    Digital PR in trade and business media, a place on credible "best of" lists, and videos with full transcripts.

Questions

What is the difference between GEO and SEO?

SEO aims to rank a page in a list of search results. GEO aims to get your brand named and cited inside the AI-written answer. GEO builds on good SEO, but it also depends on reviews, forums, Wikipedia and press, because AI engines read those sources before they answer.

Is ChatGPT SEO the same as GEO or AEO?

Broadly, yes. ChatGPT SEO, answer engine optimisation (AEO), AI search optimisation and LLM visibility all describe getting a brand into AI answers. GEO is the term used in the research literature, and it covers every engine, not just ChatGPT.

Can you guarantee my brand will appear in ChatGPT answers?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who does. The engines decide what they say. We improve the sources they rely on, measure every week and show you exactly what moved against your Week-1 baseline.

How long does GEO take to work?

It varies by category and starting point. In one real-estate programme, the visibility score went from 32% to 58% in under 2.5 months. A one-quarter pilot with a go or stop decision at month 3 is a practical way to find out for your brand.

AI engines publish no search volume, so how do you know which questions matter?

We build a prompt universe from your SEO data, your own enquiries, a map of your category and a live baseline from the engines. We rank each question by demand and winnability. We never invent a search volume figure.

Will GEO work be compliant for a regulated business?

Yes. Everything passes brand, legal and regulatory sign-off before it goes live, including RERA, ASCI and SEBI checks where they apply. We use no fabricated reviews or figures, and our data handling is aligned with India's DPDP Act.
