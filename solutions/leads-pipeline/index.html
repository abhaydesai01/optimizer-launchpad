The short answer

Organic lead generation brings in buyers through channels you own and earn rather than paid ads. Attribution for AI search traces each deal back to where demand began, such as an AI answer, a review, a forum thread or a video, so budgets follow evidence. Optimizer360 connects first mention to closed deal and reports it every week.

Why do most attribution reports miss AI search?

Buyers now do most of their research before they speak to you. They read AI answers, reviews and forum threads, and they rarely click through. When they finally arrive, your analytics records the visit as direct traffic or a brand search, and the source that convinced them disappears from the report.

That leads to a familiar mistake: cutting the organic work that created the demand, and crediting the last ad the buyer happened to click.

Where does organic demand begin?

  • An AI answer

  • A review

    A detailed review on Google or a category portal tips the decision.

  • A thread

    An open answer from your brand on Reddit or Quora settles a doubt.

  • A video

    A walkthrough or explainer shows what a page could not.

  • An owned channel

    A guide, newsletter or WhatsApp update brings a past reader back.

What do you do with attribution data?

You move money to what works. If buyers keep naming one guide or one review site, that feeder gets more investment. If a channel produces traffic but no pipeline, you can stop it with confidence. And when the board asks what organic marketing returns, you can show deals, not impressions.

The data also sharpens the rest of the engine. First-touch answers reveal new questions for GEO, and conversations from AI agents show which facts buyers needed before they booked. See what an AI visibility audit includes for the metrics behind the first scoreboard.

An honest note on method: no tool can see inside a private AI chat. Attribution for AI search combines what buyers tell you, tagged links and dated answer captures. It is strong evidence, not perfect certainty, and we show you how every number was built.

Questions

How do I track leads that come from ChatGPT?

You cannot see inside a buyer's chat, so you combine signals. Ask every new lead where they first heard of you, tag links from your owned channels, and line up enquiry trends with dated captures of what the engines were saying. Together these give a clear, defensible picture.

What is first-touch attribution?

First-touch attribution credits a deal to the place the buyer first met your brand, such as an AI answer, a review or a forum thread. It shows which channels create demand, while last-touch attribution only shows where buyers converted.

Does this replace our CRM?

No. We work with the sales records you already keep. We add the first-touch question, source tags and the link to AI answer data, and report from there.

Can organic lead generation replace paid ads?

Over time it can reduce how much you depend on them, because owned assets keep working when budgets stop. Most companies run both while the organic engine builds. Attribution shows you when the balance can shift.

How do I prove organic marketing ROI to my board?

Show closed deals traced to where demand began, alongside the AI answer and SEO scoreboards, all against a Week-1 baseline. That turns organic work from a cost line into a pipeline report.
