Solutions · Reputation

Be trusted before the call.

Buyers read reviews before they ring you. AI engines read them before they describe you. We make sure both find the real story.

Get your AI audit →Questions, answered
The short answer

Online reputation management is the steady work of earning real reviews and real press, replying to every review in public and correcting wrong facts wherever they appear. It now also shapes how AI engines describe a brand, because they read review sites, portals and media before they summarise it. Optimizer360 runs this work with drift alerts and weekly reporting.

Why does reputation now shape AI answers?

When someone asks an AI engine whether a company is reliable, the engine does not take your word for it. It reads what customers and journalists have written about you: Google reviews, category portals, marketplaces, forums and news. Your reputation becomes a sentence in the answer, positive or not.

That makes reviews and press a direct input to your AI visibility, not just a sales aid. A thin, old or unanswered review profile gives an engine little to work with, or the wrong things.

What does reputation management include?

  • A steady flow of real reviews

    We help you ask real customers at the right moment, on the sites your buyers and AI engines both read.

  • A public reply to every review

    Good or bad, every review gets a specific, approved reply. Future buyers read the reply as much as the review.

  • Fact corrections

    Wrong addresses, prices, dates or claims fixed on listings, portals and profiles, and on Wikipedia through the talk page with sources.

  • Real press

    Digital PR that earns coverage in credible media, a source engines treat as independent.

  • Drift alerts

    We watch for new wrong facts after launches, price changes and results, and flag them before they spread.

  • Sentiment in AI answers

    We track how each engine describes you, and which reviews or articles drive that tone.

How does the review management cycle run?

  1. 01

    Listen

    We bring reviews from Google and your category portals into one view, with AI answers about your brand alongside.

  2. 02

    Reply

    Drafts are written in your voice, checked for facts and compliance, and approved by your team before they are posted.

  3. 03

    Fix the cause

    Repeated complaints go to the team that can fix them. A better product earns better reviews than a better reply.

  4. 04

    Ask

    Satisfied customers are invited to share their experience, with no incentives that break platform rules.

  5. 05

    Report

    Weekly reports show review volume, ratings, reply coverage and AI sentiment against the Week-1 baseline.

What we never do: write fake reviews, pay people to pose as customers, post from fake accounts or make promotional Wikipedia edits. These tactics break platform rules and consumer advertising rules, and once found, they damage the trust you were trying to build.

How do you know reputation work is paying off?

We measure two things. First, the review profile itself: volume, recency, rating and the share of reviews with a reply. Second, what AI engines now say about you: the sentiment of each answer and which sources they cite. The Optimizer GEO platform records every cited review site and article, so you can see which ones move the answer.

Reputation also feeds the rest of the engine. Customers who leave detailed reviews are often the ones who join your community, and buyers who arrive after reading reviews show up in your pipeline data.

Questions

Do online reviews affect what ChatGPT says about my brand?

They can. AI engines read review sites, portals and forums when they describe a company, and they reflect the tone they find. A recent, well-answered review profile gives them accurate material to work with.

Can you delete negative reviews about my business?

We do not remove genuine reviews. We reply to them in public, fix the cause where we can and report reviews that clearly break a platform's own rules through the proper process. A thoughtful reply to a fair complaint often builds more trust than a spotless rating.

How can we get more genuine reviews without breaking the rules?

Ask real customers at the moment they are happiest, make it easy to leave a review on the right site, and never offer incentives that a platform prohibits. We set up that routine with your team and track how it performs each week.

Do you write or buy reviews?

No. We never write fake reviews or pay people to pose as customers. Besides being dishonest, it breaks platform and advertising rules and can undo years of trust.

How fast should a business reply to a bad review?

As soon as you can reply accurately and calmly. A quick reply shows other buyers you are listening, but a wrong or defensive reply does more harm than a short delay. Every reply we draft is checked and approved by your team.
