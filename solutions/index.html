Six things you should own, not rent

Each one is an asset on your side of the ledger. None of them disappears when a campaign ends.

  • Brand story (GEO)

    The facts AI repeats about you come from pages you write and keep true. We get you named and cited in AI answers.

  • Reputation

    A steady stream of real reviews and real press that buyers, and AI engines, both trust.

  • Feeder network

    The sites and channels you build, plus the creators, lists and newsletters you earn, all pointing buyers your way.

  • Content and community

    Real customers who discuss, defend and recommend you, in rooms you help keep alive.

  • AI agents

    Chat agents on your website, WhatsApp and Instagram that answer accurately, qualify leads and book visits, day and night.

  • Leads and pipeline

    Every deal traced back to where demand began, so you fund what works and stop what does not.

How do the six work as one engine?

Each asset feeds the next. GEO sets the facts that AI engines repeat. Reputation and the feeder network supply the independent sources those engines check before they name you. Content and community keep real conversations going in the forums engines cite most.

When demand arrives, AI agents answer it at any hour. Leads and pipeline then trace each deal back to the answer, review or thread where it began. That evidence tells you which of the other five to fund next, and the loop starts again.

Who does the work?

AI agents do the repetitive work at scale: scanning engines, drafting, checking facts and logging every change. Six specialist teams own the results: brand strategy, GEO strategy, technical, content, digital PR and programme management. They review the KPIs every week with your CMO.

Nothing goes live without brand, legal and regulatory sign-off, including SEBI and ASCI checks where they apply. You can see the tooling behind this on the Optimizer GEO platform page, and the sectors we cover on industries.

What we won't promise: guaranteed rankings or a fixed spot in any AI answer. The engines decide. We do the rigorous work, and every week we show you what moved and what did not.

Questions

What does an organic marketing agency do differently from a performance marketing agency?

A performance agency buys reach through ads, and that reach stops when the budget stops. An organic marketing agency builds assets you keep: accurate pages AI engines quote, real reviews, owned channels, communities and attribution data. The two can run side by side, but only the organic work compounds.

Do I need all six solutions at once?

No. You can start with one. A GEO baseline is a sensible first step because it shows which buyer questions you already win, which you lose and which sources decide them. That tells you which of the other five to add next.

Do you only work with companies in India?

India is our home market. We also work with companies in the United States and Mauritius.

How long does organic marketing take to show results?

It depends on your category and starting point, and we never guarantee a result. In one real-estate programme, the visibility score across 8 AI engines rose from 32% to 58% in under 2.5 months from the Week-1 baseline. We report against that baseline every week so you can judge progress yourself.

Which industries do you work in?

Nine: real estate and hospitality, financial services and investor relations, retail and consumer brands, food and FMCG, B2B and professional services, technology and SaaS, pharma and healthcare, manufacturing, industrial and engineering, and cinema and entertainment.
