Solutions · AI agents

Answer every buyer.

Buyers ask questions at midnight and on Sundays. An agent that answers accurately and books the next step means none of that interest goes cold.

Get your AI audit →Questions, answered
The short answer

AI chat agents are assistants on your website, WhatsApp and Instagram that answer buyer questions accurately, qualify each lead and book a call or visit, at any hour. Optimizer360 builds them on the same verified facts used in your GEO work, so the answer in your chat matches the answer buyers saw in an AI engine.

Why does reply speed decide which leads you win?

Interest fades fast. Research published in Harvard Business Review in 2011 found that companies replying to an enquiry within an hour were about 7 times more likely to qualify the lead than those that waited longer. Most enquiries do not arrive during office hours, and most sales teams cannot reply to all of them within the hour.

An AI agent replies in seconds, at any time, and hands your team a qualified lead with the conversation attached.

Where do the agents work?

  • Website

    A chat on your key pages that answers product, policy and process questions from approved facts and books the next step.

  • WhatsApp

    A WhatsApp AI agent that replies where many Indian buyers prefer to talk, and keeps the thread for your team.

  • Instagram

    Replies to direct messages from people who found you through posts, reels or creators.

What does an agent do in a conversation?

  • Answers accurately

    Uses only your verified facts, and says so plainly when it does not know.

  • Qualifies

    Asks the questions your sales team would ask, such as need, timeline, budget range and location.

  • Books

    Offers a call or site visit and confirms the time with the buyer.

  • Hands over

    Passes the lead to the right person with a short summary, so nobody has to ask the same questions twice.

Why does accuracy matter more than a clever reply?

A chatbot that invents a price, a possession date or a feature does more damage than no chatbot at all. Our agents draw on the same fact pages we build for GEO, checked and approved by your team. If a question falls outside those facts, the agent says it will check and hands over to a person.

Regulated claims follow the same rules as the rest of our work: RERA, ASCI and SEBI checks before anything goes live, and data handling aligned with India's DPDP Act.

How do we set up an AI agent?

  1. 01

    Gather facts

    We collect and verify the answers buyers need: products, specifics, policies, locations and timings.

  2. 02

    Design the flow

    With your sales team we agree the qualifying questions, what counts as a good lead and when to hand over.

  3. 03

    Test

    We run the agent against real questions from your own enquiries and fix every weak answer.

  4. 04

    Launch

    The agent goes live on the channels you choose, starting with the busiest.

  5. 05

    Review weekly

    We read conversations, update facts and tune the flow, and report bookings and qualified leads.

How do agents connect to the rest of the engine?

Every conversation is a record of what buyers actually ask. Those questions feed the prompt universe we use to plan GEO work, so your content answers the questions people really have. Each agent also asks, politely, where the buyer first heard of you, which feeds the first-touch data in leads and pipeline.

For sectors with high-value, considered purchases, such as real estate, booking the visit is often the moment that matters most.

What our agents won't do: pretend to be human, invent prices or availability, or push a buyer who has said no. Every agent says it is an AI assistant, and a person is always one step away.

Questions

Can a WhatsApp AI agent book site visits or sales calls?

Yes. The agent offers available slots, confirms the time with the buyer and passes the booking and conversation summary to your team. We agree the booking rules with you before launch.

Will an AI chatbot make up answers to customer questions?

Ours are built to answer only from facts you have approved. When a question falls outside them, the agent says it will check and hands the conversation to a person. We review conversations weekly to catch and fix weak answers.

Does the agent tell people it is an AI?

Yes, always. Buyers trust a clear AI assistant more than one that pretends to be a person, and honesty here matches how we work everywhere else.

What happens to customer data from the chats?

Data handling is aligned with India's DPDP Act. We agree with you what is collected, why, where it is stored and who can see it, and we collect only what qualification needs.

How is this different from a normal website chatbot?

Many chatbots follow fixed scripts or answer from whatever text they can find. Our agents work from verified facts, qualify against criteria your sales team set, book the next step, and feed what they learn back into your GEO and attribution work.
