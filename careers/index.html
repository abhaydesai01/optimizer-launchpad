The short answer

Optimizer360 hires into brand strategy, GEO strategy, technical and engineering, content, digital PR, programme management and AI agents. We do not post fixed job listings on this page. To apply, email abhay@optimizer360.ai with the subject "Careers:" followed by the team name, for example "Careers: Digital PR", and send work you are proud of.

Straight up: this page has no job listings, salaries or perks, because we would rather not publish what we have not decided. Write to us about the team you want to join, and we will tell you where things stand.

How do we work?

Every programme runs the same loop: Scan, Diagnose, Fix, Prove. AI agents track 8 engines and record every answer. People decide what to fix, ship it and log it with its date. Then we re-measure and report weekly against the Week-1 baseline.

Each team owns its KPIs and reviews them every week with the client's CMO. We work inside clear lines: no fake reviews, no astroturfing, community posts made openly as the brand, Wikipedia only through talk pages with sources, and sign-off before anything goes live.

What do we look for?

  • Accuracy

    You check facts before you publish them, and you care when something is wrong.

  • Clear writing

    Short sentences, plain words, answers first. It is how engines and people both read.

  • Comfort with data

    You can read a baseline, spot what moved and say what it means.

  • Curiosity about AI search

    You want to know why an engine named one brand and not another.

  • Ethics you keep

    You would rather lose a quick win than post a fake review.

  • Ownership

    You ship, log what you shipped and own the result.

Questions

Do I need GEO experience to apply?

No. Generative engine optimisation is a young field. We look for accuracy, clear writing, comfort with data and curiosity about how AI engines choose what to say.

Are there open roles listed with salaries?

We do not publish job listings, salaries or perks on this page. Email abhay@optimizer360.ai with the subject "Careers:" followed by the team name, and we will tell you where things stand.

What should I send with my application?

Send work you are proud of, such as writing, a campaign, code or an analysis, with a short note on what you did and what it achieved. A CV is welcome, but the work matters more.

Where does Optimizer360 work?

Our home market is India, and we also work with brands in the United States and Mauritius. Tell us where you are based when you write.
